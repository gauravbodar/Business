# CALL SCRIPT

## Opening

> Hi, my name is Gaurav. I'm setting up a recurring Australian aluminium scrap export supply arrangement to a manufacturing operation in Gujarat, India.

> We're looking for approximately one 20-foot container per month initially, around 20 tonnes, with potential to scale significantly if the quality and supply are consistent.

> I'm looking for a recycler or processor who can supply consistent graded aluminium scrap suitable for export.

> Do you currently have export-grade aluminium available, and who handles your wholesale or commercial sales?

---

# If Connected to Sales/Trading

Say:

> We are particularly interested in aluminium wire, aluminium cable, 6063 extrusion/section, aluminium wheels, heatsinks and clean cast aluminium.

> Could you tell me which grades you regularly have available, your approximate monthly volume, and your current AUD price per kilogram?

---

# Questions — Record Every Answer

### 1. Grade
- What exact aluminium grade/material is available?
- Is it clean/segregated?
- What contamination limits apply?
- Can you provide photos/specification?

### 2. Price
- Current AUD/kg?
- Is this **ex-yard**, delivered, or another basis?
- Is the price fixed or linked to a market/index?
- How often does the price change?

### 3. Volume
- How many tonnes can you supply per month?
- Can you commit to ~20 tonnes/month?
- Can volume increase later?

### 4. Container
- Can you load a 20-foot container?
- Do you arrange loading?
- Typical container weight?
- Bale/loose material?

### 5. Documentation
Ask whether they can provide:
- Commercial invoice
- Packing list
- Weight certificate
- Material/grade description
- Origin information
- Export documentation
- Any required scrap declarations/certificates

### 6. Payment
Ask:

> What payment terms do you normally offer for a new commercial customer?

Do **not** volunteer an advance-payment commitment.

### 7. Inspection
Ask:

> Can we inspect the material before the first shipment?

---

# If They Ask: "What Price Are You Looking For?"

Say:

> I'm comparing several grades and suppliers at the moment, so I don't want to give you an arbitrary target price. If you give me your current price by grade and the available monthly volume, I can evaluate the material properly.

---

# If They Ask: "Why India?"

Say:

> We have an established downstream manufacturing requirement in Gujarat, and we're developing a recurring Australian supply channel.

Keep it simple. Do not disclose the full business structure.

---

# If They Say They Already Export

Say:

> That's actually useful for us. I'm interested in understanding whether you have any additional or surplus volume available beyond your existing export commitments. We are looking for a reliable recurring supply rather than a one-off purchase.

---

# If They Say They Don't Have 20 Tonnes

Say:

> No problem. What volume can you reliably supply each month, and which grades do you have the most of?

A 5–10 tonne supplier may still become part of the 200-tonne/month supply network.

---
